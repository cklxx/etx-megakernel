# Can a kernel swap reach 300 tok/s at batch 1? (2026-10-11)

Qwen3-30B-A3B bf16, one MI300X, batch 1, context 1024. CPU-only review: no GPU run, no code change.
Tags: `[算]` my arithmetic, `[估]` my estimate, `[unknown]` not measured. `results_*` paths are in the main checkout `~/code/etx/`.
`V/` is `~/code/vllm` (`rocm/release/0.27.0`, f46a9dfe2); the timed image runs 0.27.1 (`results_vlbest2_2026-10-10/a_r1_S3.log:9`).
Shorthand: `MODEL` = `docs/qwen/MODEL-vllm-kernels.md`, `PLAN` = `docs/core/PLAN-generic-decoder-2026-10-08.md`, `FLOORS` = `docs/qwen/FLOORS-2026-10-08.md`; bare `a_r*_S*.log` and `etx_r1.log` are in `results_vlbest2_2026-10-10/`.

**Answer: no.** If every phase reached its upper-bound gain, the step would be about 3.30 ms, or 303 tok/s `[算]`.
Those bounds price LDS, VALU and MFMA work at zero, so no real kernel reaches them (`docs/core/PLAN-generic-decoder-2026-10-08.md:199`).
The measured alternatives on this card are no faster than ETX's current phase bodies. 300 tok/s needs work that does not exist yet.

## 1. Anchors, rechecked

**The tuned-table trap.** A Triton JSON table is read only by `get_moe_configs` (`V/vllm/model_executor/layers/fused_moe/fused_moe.py:1105,1133`), on the **TritonExperts** backend.

## 5. Go / no-go and the cheapest decisive experiment

**No-go for 300 tok/s in the next two working days.** The best case needs about 1.17 ms, every bound at its floor. The cited alternatives match ETX or are slower, and the two big unknowns (AITER MoE and attention inside ETX) each need importer work and a new certificate.
A realistic two-day result is lever 1 plus lever 2: up to about 0.19 ms, about 4.28 ms = 234 tok/s `[估]`.

**Cheapest experiment that could change the answer:** one rocprofv3 kernel trace of vLLM with AITER on. It is one run of about 15 VM minutes, roughly $0.50 `[估]`.
It measures AITER's MoE stage 1 + stage 2 at M = 1 against ETX's 26.9 µs. Experts are 35% of the step, and the gap to their 17.56 µs floor is 0.71 ms `[算]`:
`rocprofv3 --kernel-trace --output-format csv -d $HOME/sess/prof_aiter -- env VLLM_ROCM_USE_AITER=1 python3 examples/vllm_llm/vllm_profile.py run --model $HOME/llm/qwen3-30b-a3b/hf --golden $HOME/llm/qwen3-30b-a3b/golden_c1024.txt`, then `python3 examples/vllm_llm/vllm_profile.py analyze $HOME/sess/prof_aiter`.
Pre-registered threshold: if AITER's two MoE stages sum to ≤ 21 µs per layer, lever 6 can recover ≥ 0.28 ms `[算]`, and 300 tok/s becomes a multi-week target. If they sum to > 24 µs, the MoE swap is closed, and 300 tok/s needs a new MoE tile design.

## 4. What changed, and the next measurement

The mixed-tile wall fusion claim now passes its own correctness and timing gates, despite the predicted miss.
Hardware provenance disproves the bare-metal assumption. The primary system comparison remains unresolved because
vLLM's difference-of-windows estimator varies, while ETX is stable (evidence in sections above).
The unchanged five-round rule cannot resolve this observed sample; this does not prove that every future sample on the machine must fail.

Two candidates for a future, separately pre-registered measurement:

- **More paired rounds, with a newly specified statistical rule.** No finite number can outvote one opposite-sign
  outlier under the existing all-negative and max-minus-min rules (SPEC §7; observed exception:
  `results_nvref_2026-10-09/report.log:15-16`). [E, conditional design calculation] For independent signs under a fair-sign null,
  allowing one exception gives one-sided p = `(n+1)/2^n`: **8 total rounds** gives **9/256 = 0.03515625**, below a proposed
  **0.05** level; **7** gives **8/128 = 0.0625**. To match the original five-unanimous sign evidence **1/32** (SPEC §11),
  use at least **9 total rounds**, **10/512 = 0.01953125**. These are design calculations, not logged measurements or
  a power guarantee. Additional outliers or dependent signs invalidate this example; a robust magnitude/uncertainty
  criterion must also be registered because adding rounds cannot shrink an already observed range. Do not reclassify this session.
- **Find the outlier trigger**, the more direct next unknown: retain separate full/single-token wall windows, instrument
  their warmup/JIT and scheduling boundaries, and test whether window-specific delay explains the opposite excursions
  (`results_nvref_2026-10-09/vllm_r2.json:16-19`, `results_nvref_2026-10-09/vs_r4.json:16-19`).
  Use a correctness-gated diagnostic before another system verdict run; no cause is established yet.

Host work remains measurable but is not the deciding uncertainty for this verdict. The profiled all-step means and
decode-only device median cover different populations (`results_nvref_2026-10-09/hostprof.log:2119-2120`);
SPEC §11's async/resident split still requires a matching-step device mean. Sampling/output/scheduling scope also
remains unmatched (`results_nvref_2026-10-09/report.log:24`). No NVIDIA comparison follows from this run.

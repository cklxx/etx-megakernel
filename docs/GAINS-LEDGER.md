# Gains ledger

This ledger keeps the project's gain claims, their baselines and their decision rules together without choosing between conflicting numbers. A row is **clear** only when its number, source, threshold and both actions are present; **half-clear** means some are present but a condition, action or reconciliation is missing; **unclear** means no usable quantitative decision is sourced. [M] means measured-derived and [E] means estimated, including bounds and unmeasured expectations; a decision threshold is a rule, not an observation.

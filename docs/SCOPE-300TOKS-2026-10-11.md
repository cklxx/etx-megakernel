- **"the existing switch"**: the gate_up→down K-chunk. ETX's DeepSeek graph uses one chunk (`examples/dsv2lite/graph.py:5-6`). Fleet has `--k-chunk` (`fleet:src/host/taskgraph.py:808`), but `kExpertKChunk = 2048` is at least moe_inter, which means one chunk (`fleet:src/runtime/fleet_types.h:20-21`). moe_inter is 1408 (`fleet:src/kernels/expert.h:15`), so four pieces are 352 rows.

## 4. Ceiling check for 300 tok/s (`CLAUDE.md` section 2)

**Qwen.**
- Bytes per step are 6.1904 GB, and the bandwidth floor is 1.44 ms at 4.3 TB/s (`docs/FLOORS-2026-10-08.md:93,215`). The serial device floor is 2.84 ms (`:212`).
- The step is now 4.471 ms on the wall [M], so the target needs 1.341x.
- At 3.333 ms the step would stream 1.86 TB/s, which is 43% of 4.3 TB/s; today it streams 32% (arithmetic). The highest public batch-1 MoE figure on gfx942 is 36% (`docs/SURVEY-2026-09-29.md:68-70`).
- The upper bounds of the items, per step, are: top-k 343 us, prep/attn/reduce 521 us, MoE 429 us, qkv 199 us and o_proj 14 us (`FLOORS:263-270`), plus host 100 us (`GAINS-LEDGER.md:80`). The total is 1.606 ms.
- Reaching 300 tok/s therefore needs 71% of every bound. Those bounds come from an older 5.08 ms device trace (`FLOORS:211`), and FLOORS's realised gains have been small (`FLOORS:299-304`) [E].
- The 278 us of router excess (`FLOORS:270`) is in no item.
- **Verdict:** the ten items do not plausibly reach 300 tok/s on Qwen. The missing lever is fewer bytes per token, or more than one token per weight read: FP8 weights, or multi-token prediction or speculative decoding [I]. Each changes the claim in `CLAUDE.md` section 1.

**DeepSeek.**
- The floor is 4.935 GB modelled, 1.15 ms, or 5.20 GB measured, 1.21 ms, at 4.3 TB/s (`fleet:docs/STATUS.md:43,372`; arithmetic). The step is now 3.593 ms, so the target needs 1.078x, a cut of 0.260 ms; 3.333 ms is 36% of 4.3 TB/s.
- D1 and D2 only bring safe up to relaxed. D3 and D5 measured negative or neutral before.
- D4 is bounded at about 5% (`docs/ETX_Technical_Design.md:1046`), about 0.18 ms of 3.593 ms [E], which is short of 0.260 ms.
- **Missing lever:** the two per-layer folds, about 0.26 ms per token (`fleet:docs/STATUS.md:522-525`), which are not in the list.
- ETX's relaxed 3.474 ms needs only 0.141 ms, but it comes from another session and from the relaxed setting.

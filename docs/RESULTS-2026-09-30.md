## hw_bench stream: the hardware floor for our exact phase shapes

296 workgroups, no events, no tasks, just reading N bytes each. `contig` = one contiguous block per workgroup
(what our GEMV does); `rows` = 4 KB row r to worker r % W (interleaved).

| shape | bytes per WG | contig span | rows span | contig TB/s | rows TB/s |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| router | 896 B | 3.35 | 3.17 | 0.08 | 0.08 |
| o_proj | 28 KB | 7.05 | 4.87 | 1.20 | 1.74 |
| q/kv | 50 KB | 10.10 | 6.17 | 1.50 | 2.46 |
| down | 156 KB | 18.74 | 16.29 | 2.52 | 2.90 |
| gate_up | 312 KB | 33.86 | 31.54 | 2.79 | 3.00 |
| 1 MB | 1 MB | 91.49 | 87.29 | 3.39 | 3.56 |

Two facts this settles:

1. **A phase of 296 workgroups costs about 3 us no matter how few bytes it moves.** router reads 896 B per
   workgroup and still takes 3.2 us. With 8 phases per layer over 48 layers, that floor alone is about 1.15 ms
   per token, independent of bandwidth. The per-task tax the old model fitted is really a *per-phase* floor,
   which is why cutting tasks, cutting events and prefetching all changed nothing.
2. **The row-to-workgroup mapping is worth 30-60% on the dense shapes.** Interleaved beats contiguous everywhere
   (o_proj 1.20 -> 1.74 TB/s, q/kv 1.50 -> 2.46). `dsl_gemv_rows` takes a contiguous `[r0, r1)` per task, so we
   are on the slow pattern. Switching the mapping is worth about 10.8 us per layer, 0.52 ms per token (9%).

## hw_bench lfx: bandwidth by XCD count (new; nobody has published this)

WG-median based: 1 XCD 0.85 TB/s, 2 XCDs 1.21 (0.60 each), 4 XCDs 2.57 (0.64), 8 XCDs 3.57 (0.45). Scaling is
sublinear: 8 XCDs give 4.2x one XCD, not 8x. One XCD alone beats AMD's published ~0.5 TB/s for NPS1.
The span-vs-median gap at 8 XCDs (36.84 vs 21.76 us) is 15 us of workgroup start skew.

## hw_bench sdat: the data as the signal is not faster here

| protocol | last store -> consumer sees it |
|---|---|
| coherent store (sc0 sc1) + coherent poll | 2.6-3.8 us |
| plain store + buffer_wbl2, coherent poll | 3.2-13.8 us |
| plain store + buffer_inv sc1, plain load | hangs (127 / 3552 consumers stuck) |
| our events (trace / evx) | 0.4 / 1.8 and 2.2 / 3.9 |

Kog's 0.8 us is not reproduced. Caveat: each consumer rescans the whole 4 KB vector with uncached loads every
round, which is expensive by construction; a fair test would poll only the slice it needs. Either way, building
the sentinel hand-off now is not justified. Separately confirmed: plain stores + buffer_inv sc1 really does read
stale cross-XCD L2 (it hangs), so a producer-side wbl2 is mandatory, as the Fleet paper states.

## What this changes

- The cost model must be rebuilt around a per-phase floor (about 3 us for 296 workgroups) plus a bandwidth term
  that depends on the row mapping, not around per-task costs and event counts.
- autoconf searched with that wrong model, which is how router ended up with 304 tasks to move 0.5 MB
  (11.7 us per layer, 0.56 ms per token).
- Known bug: --check-dsl asserts "launch 573 is not topk" on build 2 (the non-fold path's launch indexing).

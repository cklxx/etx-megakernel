# 300 tok/s: certain plan, or the one number that blocks it (2026-10-12)

**Answer: there is no certain plan.** Even with every surviving lever at 100% of its ceiling, the
priced rows fall **0.142 ms** short of the **0.847 ms** cut needed to go from 4.180 to 3.3333 ms/step.
At the values that tests have actually shown, the shortfall is **0.155 ms**. The single blocking number
is **G**: the stamped above-floor protocol time in gap rows 9-10, which must be **≥ 0.088 ms** (S1).
The cheapest measurement that settles it is a rerun of tranche A on the fixed stamp build (54ea65c):
**15 GPU minutes, $0.50**. A negative result closes the route to 300 for this architecture. The
2026-10-11 hour session tried to measure G and failed. It also measured the kept build **0.80 ms
slower** than the 4.180 anchor. That makes a fresh same-session anchor a precondition of every number
in this document (sections 3 and 6).

**Condition.** One MI300X VF, Qwen3-30B-A3B, bf16, batch 1, context 1024, 32 generated tokens. Times
are ms per decode step on host wall time unless marked device. The anchor is KEY32-on fused,
**4.180 ms wall [M]** / **4.045 ms device [M]** (`results_qwen300_2026-10-11/job2_run4.log:144-145`).
Need = `4.180 - 3.3333 = 0.8467 ms` (`docs/KEY32-MEASURED-2026-10-11.md:66-68`). KEY32's **0.231 ms
[M]** is already inside the anchor and is not counted again.

**Labels** (as in `docs/MASTER-TABLE-2026-10-11.md:10-12`): **[M]** measured, with a file; **[E]**
estimate, with its arithmetic; **[R]** residual of an [M] interval after an [E] model is subtracted;
**[U]** unknown, which adds nothing to any total. Every ceiling below is a pool shrinking to its floor.
No lever has been shown to reach that.

already below it, with a minimum of 0.68 us (X7:164-165). A lower floor would raise B2/B3. It would not
make the untested share tested.

## 4. Falsification: what kills the route, in order

**What G is.** For each protocol atom class (P1 fetch, P10 arrive, P5/P11 observation, P4/P6/P7 setup
including touch), G is the critical-path time by which the stamped interval exceeds that atom's idle
price. A class counts only at ≥ 0.625 us/layer (P300:301). G is an upper bound on what a protocol change
could recover, not a saving (INB:168-175). S1's 0.088 is `0.324 − 0.236`: below it, even attention +
reduce at its full ceiling cannot meet the whole-target rule.

**Why the hour session gave no G, and why its retained data cannot.** Tranche A analysis stopped at
`etx/tools/inbody.py:97` with "invalid success order/clock for task 1" in both attempts (HOUR/W
`A_analysis.log`). The cause is on the device. `etx_inbody_tick()` read the `s_memrealtime` result
before the asynchronous SMEM return. The fix adds `s_waitcnt lgkmcnt(0)` inside the asm (18495c8,
`etx/runtime/include/etx/inbody.h:7-9`) and is merged in 54ea65c. The analyzer now names a missing poll
stamp before the order check (296575b). The retained `raw/inbody.bin` (17.9 MB) was written by the
unfixed build. Its timestamps are not trustworthy, so a CPU re-analysis can diagnose the failure but
cannot produce G [E, from the fix's stated cause].

**Perturbation risk for the rerun [E].** The hour session's stamps build ran at 5.253 ms wall / 5.095
ms device, against the kept build's 4.981 / 4.840 (HOUR/W `report.json`, `arms.*.gate_wall`). That is
+0.255 ms device [M]. Over 48 layers × 9 roles that is about 0.59 us per critical body [E, role count
assumed], above the 0.5 us limit. The registered test is the in-job OFF/ON comparison (F1), not this
build-to-build delta, so this does not fail F1 by itself. But the added `s_waitcnt` can only add stall
per stamp. If F1 fails, the rerun yields no G, and a sparser-stamp build is the next step.

## 6. Certainty

**Certain (arithmetic on cited numbers):**

- The rows already priced (group A, 0.705) cannot reach the 0.847 need at any realization. The gap is
  0.142 ms, or 0.152 with the measured reset submit.
- With only tested values, the plan is ≥ 0.155 ms short, even with every untested ceiling at 100%.
- Built candidates reach at most 3.913 ms (255.6 tok/s), and none has a KEEP pair yet.
- MoE's only named mechanism closes by the bar. No measured attention + reduce alternative is faster.
- KEY32's 0.231 is spent and is not counted again. H (0.073 [M]) and the 0.285 TOUCH stake are not in
  any sum.

**Not certain (every [E] and [R] above):**

- Whether any of the 0.386 ms untested protocol share in rows 9-10 exists as above-floor time. G
  answers this.
- Whether a legal attention + reduce reaches ≤ 7.69 us/layer (X5).
- Whether the next VM runs the kept build at 4.18 or 4.98 ms. The cause of the 0.80 ms drift is [U].
- Whether the ceilings, priced on the 10-11 trace, hold on that VM.

**Verdict.** There is no certain plan. The **single blocking number is G**, the stamped above-floor
protocol time in gap rows 9-10:

- **Threshold:** G ≥ 0.088 ms (1.833 us/layer) to continue (S1). Then G + R2 + H ≥ 0.324 ms (S2).
- **Cheapest settling measurement:** tranche A rerun on the fixed stamp build at 54ea65c, **15 GPU
  minutes, $0.50**, after the $0 CPU gates of section 5. The same 15 minutes also re-measures the anchor
  (F0), which decides whether 0.847 is still the need.
- **If negative** (G < 0.088, or F0 > 4.70 ms): close the route to 300 tok/s for this architecture.
  Record rows 9-10 at their stamped values and fund no larger system build. What remains is
  intermediate work under the ordinary lever rule, at most 3.913 ms on the 10-11 basis, which needs its
  own approval (P300:320).
- **If positive:** it is necessary, not sufficient. A positive G only keeps the route open into tranche
  B. Because G is an upper bound and not a saving, 300 tok/s becomes certain only when F7 passes:
  five complete rounds ≤ 3.3333 ms host wall with fresh 97/97 and 32/32, at a cumulative $2.22.
- **If F1 fails** (stamp perturbation): NOT-COMPLETE again, at $0.50. The next step is a sparser-stamp
  build checked on the CPU first, not a third attempt with the same build.

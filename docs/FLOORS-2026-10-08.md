# Hardware floors of the Qwen3-30B-A3B decode phases, 2026-10-08

Date: 2026-10-08. Status: CPU estimate. No GPU measurement supports a new number in this file.
This file gives a hardware floor for each phase of one decode layer.
`python -m etx.tools.floors` reads the tables below. `tests/test_floors.py` checks the tool.
Section 2.2 of `docs/PLAN-generic-decoder-2026-10-08.md` uses the results.

## Rule

No phase floor comes from a measured task body. A body-derived floor makes the serial floor equal to the measured time.
Each phase gets one source tag:

1. `hops x RTT`: count the dependent global-memory round trips on the critical task from the tile source. Multiply the count by the round trip.
2. `probe`: use the hw_stream achievable time of the phase.
3. `probe + hops x RTT`: add the round trips that must finish before the stream starts. The probe kernel does not contain them.
4. `bytes/BW`: only the byte floor applies.
5. `unknown`: no hop count and no probe exist. The phase adds only its bytes divided by the bandwidth. The tool lists the phase.

Loads that can be in flight together count as one round trip.
The minimum count assumes that the compiler issues each independent load as early as the source permits.
The maximum count follows the program order of the source, or the ISA evidence where it exists.
No ISA of the round11 build exists. Thus, each count is a range.

## Count rules

1. The ETX body ends with `ETX_RELEASE_DOMAIN`. Source: `ae1529c etx/codegen/kernel.py:320`. On gfx942 it is `s_waitcnt vmcnt(0)`. Source: `etx/machine/arch/gfx942.yaml:28`.
2. On gfx9, `vmcnt` also counts stores. Thus the drain waits for the last store acknowledgement. Source: `examples/vllm_llm/ops/hip.md:40`.
3. A DSL store with `-DETX_DSL_COH` is a system-scope atomic store past L2. Its drain is one coherent hop. Source: `ae1529c etx/ops/coh.py:26-28`.
4. An imported kernel uses plain stores into L2. Its drain is one cached hop.
5. The argument block comes through scalar loads: the table pointer, then the block base. Source: `ae1529c etx/runtime/include/etx/import.h:106-108`. Then the fields follow. These are 2 to 3 cached hops.
6. The event poll, the acquire and the arrive sit in the handoff term. They are not in a body.
7. A probe phase takes no argument hop and no drain. The probe rate already includes the cost of one task.

## Chains

### prep

The k head wave, wave G, is the critical wave. It has the longest chain. Source file: `ae1529c etx/ops/attnprep.py`.

1. Cached: argument block, lines 29-41. 2 to 3 hops.
2. Coherent: the head row, 2 per-lane atomic loads, line 50. 1 hop.
3. Coherent: the position, line 57, and the norm weight, lines 54-55. The source puts them after the wave sum at line 52. 0 to 1 hop.
4. Cached: cos and sin, lines 63-64. The address depends on the position. 1 hop.
5. Coherent: the slot, line 70. It follows the row stores of line 68 in program order. The stores need the rope result. 1 hop.
6. Coherent: the drain of the k cache stores, lines 73-74. 1 hop.

Round 4 measured +0.044 ms when the source moved the position load forward. Source: line 57. Thus the compiled order likely had the separate hop of step 3.
Round 22 found that the body is bound by the request rate. Each per-lane 2-byte coherent access is one request. The floor does not include this rate.

### attn

The kernel is vLLM's. The ISA chain is `examples/vllm_llm/ops/hip.md:389`.

1. Cached: argument block and kernel fields, `AT:350-361`. 2 to 4 hops.
2. Coherent: `seq_lens[0]`, `AT:362`. 1 hop.
3. Coherent: the K block numbers, `AT:438`. 1 hop.
4. Coherent: Q, `AT:446-470`. The ISA waits for step 3 with `vmcnt(0)` first. Source: `examples/vllm_llm/ops/hip.md:353`. 1 hop.
5. Coherent: K, 16 loads per lane, `AT:497-515`. About 8 loads are in flight. Source: `examples/vllm_llm/ops/hip.md:356`. 1 to 2 hops.
6. Coherent: the V block numbers, `AT:550-551`. The ISA issues them after most K data arrives. Source: `examples/vllm_llm/ops/hip.md:358`. 0 to 1 hop.
7. Coherent: V, 16 loads in flight, `AT:555-579`. 1 hop.
8. Cached: 2 serial scratch reloads. Source: `examples/vllm_llm/ops/hip.md:364`. 2 hops.
9. Cached: the drain of the `tmp_out`, `max_logits` and `exp_sums` stores, `AT:780-781` and `AT:906`. 1 hop.

The acquire before an imported task invalidates L2. Thus each data hop misses L2.
The 7 barriers and 4 shuffle steps are not in the floor.

### reduce

The kernel is vLLM's. Each task runs 8 blocks of 128 threads at the same time. The ISA chain is `examples/vllm_llm/ops/hip.md:420`.

1. Cached: argument block and kernel fields, `AT:1436-1441`. 2 to 3 hops.
2. Coherent: `seq_lens[0]`, `AT:1442`. 1 hop.
3. Coherent: wave 0 loads `max_logits` and `exp_sums` together, `AT:1469` and `AT:1489`. Source: `examples/vllm_llm/ops/hip.md:406`. 1 hop.
4. Coherent: the `tmp_out` loads, `AT:1538`. hip.md counts them with step 3. Source: `examples/vllm_llm/ops/hip.md:420`. 0 to 1 hop.
5. Cached: the drain of the output store, `AT:1619`. 1 hop.

The ISA lines put the `tmp_out` loads at `:1119-1126`, after the wave 0 shuffles at `:985-1064`. Source: `examples/vllm_llm/ops/hip.md:407-408`.
Thus wave 0 can pay step 4 as a separate hop. The maximum counts it.

### router

The source is `ae1529c etx/ops/gemv.py`. The critical task is a task with a row. 128 of the 256 tasks have no row.

1. Cached: argument block, lines 378-380 of the emitted entry and `dsl_args` at lines 220-227. 2 to 3 hops.
2. Coherent: the folded norm, line 236 and line 238. Each thread has 2 elements because K = 2048 and the task has 1024 threads. 1 to 4 hops.
3. Cached: the norm weight, line 245. It follows the barrier in `etx_block_sum` at line 242. 1 hop.
4. Cached: the weight row. The touch hook loads it into L2 during the wait. Source: line 298. 1 hop.
5. Coherent: the drain of the output store, line 321. 1 hop.

In the maximum, each element pass loads the row, waits, then loads the residual. The row loop at line 236 has a runtime trip count.
The round37 ISA shows this serial order for the later 16-byte fold. Source: `docs/TILES-2026-10-07.md:45-46`.

### topk

The source is `ae1529c etx/ops/topk.py`. Wave 0 does all work. Line 23 returns the other waves.

1. Cached: argument block, lines 24-26. 2 to 3 hops.
2. Coherent: 2 logit loads per lane, line 31. They are in flight together. 1 hop.
3. LDS only: 6 shuffle steps for the maximum, 6 for the sum, and 96 for the 8 selection rounds. Lines 32, 36 and 45-48.
4. Coherent: lane 0 stores `ids` and `tei` in each round, line 51. The minimum lets all rounds overlap. The maximum gives each round 1 hop. 0 to 8 hops.
5. Coherent: the weight stores, line 54, and the drain. 1 hop.

Round 13 removed all 24 stores and saved 0.223 ms, or 4.6 us per layer. Source: `docs/BUDGET-2026-10-01.md:299`.
That result agrees with the maximum. The LDS steps in step 3 are not in the floor.

### qkv

The source is `ae1529c etx/ops/gemv.py`. The tag is `probe + hops x RTT`. The probe is 6.63 us.

1. Coherent: the folded norm, `dsl_stage_norm`, lines 231-249. Each thread loads 8 expert rows and the residual for each of 2 elements, lines 236 and 238. 1 to 18 hops.
2. Cached: the norm weight, line 245, after the barrier at line 242. 1 hop.
3. Probe: the weight stream, line 321.

The maximum is 2 passes of 9 serial loads. The round37 ISA shows 9 serial round trips per pass for the later 16-byte form. Source: `docs/TILES-2026-10-07.md:45-46`.
Layer 0 has no fold. Its body is 6.66 us against the 6.63 us probe. Source: `docs/BUDGET-2026-10-01.md:187`.

### o_proj

The tag is `probe`. The probe is 5.30 us. The tool adds no hop.
The touch hook loads the first weight trip of each task during the wait. Source: `ae1529c etx/ops/gemv.py:298`.
Thus the body can beat the probe. The measured body is 103% of the probe rate. Source: `docs/BUDGET-2026-10-01.md:186`.

### moe_up and moe_down

The tag is `probe + hops x RTT`. The probes are 13.15 us and 7.96 us.
The weight address depends on the expert id, `ae1529c etx/ops/gemv.py:334` and line 357. The touch hook cannot load the weights early.
The activation loads of `dsl_stage`, lines 197-198, need no id. They can be in flight with the id load. 1 to 2 hops.
The routing weight load of moe_down, line 363, overlaps the stream. It is not on the chain.

## Terms outside the floor

These terms are not in any phase floor. Thus each floor is a lower bound.

1. LDS round trips: the shuffle and wave-sum steps. topk has 108 of them per task.
2. Barriers inside a task. attn has 7.
3. The request rate of per-lane 2-byte coherent accesses. prep and the folded norms use them.
4. The start delay of the critical task. The trace gives 8.63 us per layer.
5. The tail phases prologue, in_norm and argmax. Each is `unknown` and adds only its bytes.

## Findings

1. With the minimum counts, the bandwidth floor sets the overlap floor. The hop chain is only 0.93 to 1.09 ms.
2. The earlier estimate took the skeletons from the bodies. Its chain was 2.59 ms. That chain was mostly body time.
3. The gain without prefetch is 2.21 to 2.42 ms. The gain from prefetch is 0.96 to 1.38 ms. Thus the first lever is shorter critical bodies.
4. The five latency phases own the largest part of that gain. They have 1.07 to 1.20 ms of body excess.
5. The maximum counts reverse the order of the two gains. But the maximum case puts qkv above its own body. Thus it is not a valid floor.

## Limits

1. No ISA of `b_r8base` exists on this machine. The counts are ranges. A gfx942 build of `ae1529c` would fix them.
2. At 0.50 us, 18 fold hops take 9.0 us. The measured fold costs 5.18 us. Source: `docs/BUDGET-2026-10-01.md:191`.
3. At the 0.28 us median of `hw_cohload.log`, 18 hops take 5.0 us. This check agrees with 18 serial hops. It suggests that 0.50 us is high for one hop in a busy wave.
4. At 0.28 us with the minimum counts, the serial floor is 2.48 ms and the hop chain is 0.75 ms at 4.3 TB/s.
5. The probes at width 256 are interpolations with an error of about 7%.
6. The handoff term is the measured 1.00 us per edge. It is not a hop count.
7. The trace is from 2026-10-01. The shipped build has bodies 2.80 us shorter per layer. Source: `docs/BUDGET-2026-10-01.md:104-115`.

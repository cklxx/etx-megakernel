# Bringing our tiles to vLLM's: what is actually recoverable (2026-10-01, round 27)

`docs/PHASES-2026-10-01.md` identified three phases where our DSL tile is slower than vLLM's kernel.
It estimated a recoverable 0.119 ms/token against a 0.075 ms gap.
That estimate suggested that kernel parity would put ETX ahead.
This round tested each phase.
**Only 0.026 ms is recoverable, not 0.119. Parity on these three rows does not put ETX ahead of vLLM.**

Two of the three rows compare unequal work, the same defect that round 25 found in the `qkv` row and round 26 found in
`router` and `moe_down`. That is now four of seven examined rows.

## Top-k (claimed 0.025 ms): nothing to recover

Our top-k is an 8-task x-form replica, one per XCD, so each XCD's expert tasks wait on an XCD-local event. The
hypothesis was that the replica's publication cost (round 13: the body is one write-through round trip behind which
every coherent store sits) no longer earns its place now that `ETX_VL_COH` stops consumers invalidating.

**The arithmetic rejects the hypothesis before a build.**
The 8 replicas run concurrently, one per XCD. The phase costs one body with either 8 copies or 1.
Removal of replicas eliminates 7 tasks of fixed cost, about 0.2 µs by round 2's fold measurement.
It also changes the consumer wait from XCD-local to device scope, at 1.00 µs per edge in the budget.
The expected net result is worse.

**The build is also incorrect.**
`ETX_VL_NOREP=topk` plans and builds without an error: 60026 tasks against 60362, with 436 events unchanged.
But `host.hip:277` addresses top-k as `topk_w + rr * TOPK`, the copy for this XCD.
With one writer, XCDs 1–7 read a copy that nobody wrote.
The result has **19533 values beyond 2 ulps at layer 47** despite a healthy-looking build.

Replication is a plan and host contract, not a plan switch.
`model.py` now rejects `ETX_VL_NOREP=topk`, as the guards from rounds 14, 19, and 20 do for related failures.

## MoE down (claimed 0.056 ms): 0.026 ms is real

Our `moe_down` applies the routing weight in its epilogue.
vLLM's `fused_moe_down` applies it through the separate `moe_sum_k8`, which our fold deletes.
Thus, the −1.16 µs/layer comparison charges our kernel for work that the other path does elsewhere.
Round 7 bounded the combined epilogue at 1.4 µs/layer but could not separate moe_up's silu from moe_down's weight.

A down-only ablation (`DSL_ABLATE_WTS`, timing-only, wrong output by design), three interleaved runs against the
control:

| | run 1 | run 2 | run 3 | paired mean |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| control | 4.936 | 4.954 | 4.938 | — |
| no routing weight | 4.915 | 4.918 | 4.906 | **−0.030 ms** |

**The routing-weight multiply costs 0.62 µs/layer**, so the genuine GEMV deficit is 1.16 − 0.62 = **0.54 µs/layer =
0.026 ms/token**. Note the paired row in `PHASES` already credits us +4.10 µs/layer for "MoE down + sum", because
deleting `moe_sum_k8` and its handoff is worth far more than the multiply costs.

## Input norm (claimed 0.038 ms): not recoverable without undoing a larger win

The row compares our folded `qkv` against their `in_norm` + `qkv`. Isolating the fold with layer 0, which the model
does not fold (`model.py:191`):

| | µs |
|---|---|
| our unfolded GEMV, 5120×2048 | 6.29 |
| our folded qkv | 10.04 |
| **our fold's own cost** | **3.75** |
| their `wvsplitk` | 6.75 |
| their `fused_add_rms_norm` | 2.50 |
| **their norm's own cost** | **2.50** |

Our path costs 1.25 µs/layer more for the same work. The cause is structural.
Each of our 256 qkv tasks sums the 8 expert rows and the residual.
That costs 36 KB per task, or **9.2 MB per layer**.
vLLM's 8-task norm reads the 36 KB once.

This rematerialisation is the fold's tradeoff, and it is already our better option.
Our separate `in_norm` phase measures 5.9 µs/layer in round 25.
Thus, the fold saves 2.15 µs/layer against our own alternative, despite the 1.25 cost against vLLM.
A cheaper path must avoid another read of the expert rows, which is the purpose of the `moe_sum` fold.
`PHASES` credits the folds with +0.500 ms/token overall.

## Consequence

| row | claimed | recoverable | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| top-k | 0.025 | **0** | replicas are concurrent<br> de-replicating costs a device-scope wait |
| MoE down | 0.056 | **0.026** | 0.030 of it is the routing weight vLLM applies in a phase we deleted |
| input norm | 0.038 | **0** | the deficit is the fold's redundant reads, and the fold beats our alternative |
| | 0.119 | **0.026** | |

Against a 0.075 ms gap to vLLM, **matching their kernels on these rows leaves ETX behind by about 0.05 ms**. The
`PHASES` estimate was inflated by the same unequal-work comparison it was written to prevent; its structural
conclusions are unaffected, since those were computed from paired rows.

Nothing shipped. `DSL_ABLATE_WTS` is a timing-only ablation, off by default, with its number recorded at the flag.

# Bringing our tiles to vLLM's: what is actually recoverable (2026-10-01, round 27)

`docs/PHASES-2026-10-01.md` listed three phases where our DSL tile is slower than vLLM's kernel and put the total at
0.119 ms/token against a 0.075 ms gap — which would mean matching their kernels everywhere puts ETX ahead. This round
tested each. **The recoverable total is 0.026 ms, not 0.119, and parity on these three rows does not pass vLLM.**

Two of the three rows compare unequal work, the same defect that round 25 found in the `qkv` row and round 26 found in
`router` and `moe_down`. That is now four of seven examined rows.

## Top-k (claimed 0.025 ms): nothing to recover

Our top-k is an 8-task x-form replica, one per XCD, so each XCD's expert tasks wait on an XCD-local event. The
hypothesis was that the replica's publication cost (round 13: the body is one write-through round trip behind which
every coherent store sits) no longer earns its place now that `ETX_VL_COH` stops consumers invalidating.

**The arithmetic refutes it before any build.** The 8 replicas run *concurrently*, one per XCD, so the phase costs one
body whether there are 8 copies or 1. De-replicating removes 7 tasks of fixed cost — about 0.2 µs by round 2's fold
measurement — and raises the consumers' wait from XCD-local to device scope, which the budget prices at 1.00 µs per
edge. Expected net: worse.

**The build is also incorrect, which is worth recording.** `ETX_VL_NOREP=topk` plans cleanly (60026 tasks against
60362, events unchanged at 436) and builds, but `host.hip:277` addresses the top-k as `topk_w + rr * TOPK` — "this
XCD's top-k copy" — so with one writer, XCDs 1–7 read a copy nobody wrote: **19533 values beyond 2 ulps at layer 47**,
after a build that looks healthy. The replica is a plan *and host* contract, not a plan switch. `model.py` now refuses
`ETX_VL_NOREP=topk` rather than miscompiling, in the manner of the guards from rounds 14, 19 and 20.

## MoE down (claimed 0.056 ms): 0.026 ms is real

Our `moe_down` applies the routing weight in its epilogue; vLLM's `fused_moe_down` does not — it applies it in the
separate `moe_sum_k8` that our fold deletes. So the −1.16 µs/layer kernel-against-kernel row charges us for work the
other side does elsewhere. Round 7's ablation bounded the *combined* epilogue (moe_up's silu and moe_down's weight) at
1.4 µs/layer but could not separate them.

A down-only ablation (`DSL_ABLATE_WTS`, timing-only, wrong output by design), three interleaved runs against the
control:

| | run 1 | run 2 | run 3 | paired mean |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| control | 4.936 | 4.954 | 4.938 | — |
| no routing weight | 4.915 | 4.918 | 4.906 | **−0.030 ms** |

**The routing-weight multiply costs 0.62 µs/layer**, so the genuine GEMV deficit is 1.16 − 0.62 = **0.54 µs/layer =
0.026 ms/token**. Note the paired row in `PHASES` already credits us +4.10 µs/layer for "MoE down + sum", because
deleting `moe_sum_k8` and its hand-off is worth far more than the multiply costs.

## Input norm (claimed 0.038 ms): not recoverable without undoing a larger win

The row compares our folded `qkv` against their `in_norm` + `qkv`. Isolating the fold with layer 0, which the model
does not fold (`model.py:191`):

| | µs |
|---|---|
| our unfolded GEMV, 5120×2048 | 6.29 |
| our folded qkv | 10.04 |
| **our fold's own cost** | **3.75** |
| their `wvsplitk` | 6.75 |
| their `fused_add_rms_norm` | 2.50 |
| **their norm's own cost** | **2.50** |

We are 1.25 µs/layer dearer at the same work — and the reason is structural, not a slow tile. Every one of our 256
qkv tasks sums the 8 expert rows and the residual itself: 36 KB per task, **9.2 MB per layer**, against the 36 KB
vLLM's 8-task norm reads once. That is rematerialisation, the fold's defining trade, and it is already the better of
our two options: our own separate `in_norm` phase measures 5.9 µs/layer (round 25), so folding saves 2.15 µs/layer
against *us*, even while costing 1.25 against *them*. Making it cheaper means not re-reading the expert rows, which is
the `moe_sum` fold — and `PHASES` credits the folds +0.500 ms/token overall.

## Consequence

| row | claimed | recoverable | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| top-k | 0.025 | **0** | replicas are concurrent; de-replicating costs a device-scope wait |
| MoE down | 0.056 | **0.026** | 0.030 of it is the routing weight vLLM applies in a phase we deleted |
| input norm | 0.038 | **0** | the deficit is the fold's redundant reads, and the fold beats our alternative |
| | 0.119 | **0.026** | |

Against a 0.075 ms gap to vLLM, **matching their kernels on these rows leaves ETX behind by about 0.05 ms**. The
`PHASES` estimate was inflated by the same unequal-work comparison it was written to prevent; its structural
conclusions are unaffected, since those were computed from paired rows.

Nothing shipped. `DSL_ABLATE_WTS` is a timing-only ablation, off by default, with its number recorded at the flag.
